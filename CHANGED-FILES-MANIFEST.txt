BUILT-IN RESOURCE PACK ARCHITECTURE
Punchy Built-in Animations is installed at assets/minecraft/resourcepacks/punchy.zip when the Punchy mod is enabled.
The pack is required and pinned at the bottom of the active resource-pack stack.
Player-selected resource packs are layered above it; matching custom asset paths override built-in assets.
The built-in archive contains pack.mcmeta, the default Punchy animation JSON, player geometry, and tuning template.
