PUNCHY FIRST-PERSON OWNERSHIP
Punchy now owns first-person procedural hand motion. Its runtime samples player
movement and applies the HandMotion layer to first-person arm/item poses.
Punchy Moves no longer owns first-person arm/item transforms or first-person
motion settings; it remains responsible for third-person player motion and
elytra trails. The existing Punchy action/item animation system remains intact.
