HAND MOTION SYSTEM REWRITE
Adds a separate two-hand motion engine for procedural idle breathing/jitter, spring-based hand inertia, look lag, walk/sprint cycles, jump/fall/land, flight, swimming, climbing, crawling, horse riding, and boat rowing.
Uses actual PlayerMotion inputs and per-hand first-person transforms. Keyframe clips are layered when available; procedural physics fills missing built-in clips. Punchy action clips retain arm ownership and vanilla swing is not suppressed by locomotion alone.
Built-in locomotion clip routing includes jump/land/sprint, climb/climb2, crawl, swimming, and horse riding clips where present in the built-in archive. Boat rowing uses procedural motion because the built-in archive has no dedicated rowing animation.
