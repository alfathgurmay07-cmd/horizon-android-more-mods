# Freecam+

- Native mod ID: `freecam_plus`
- Ported from `freecam_plus-1.0.1.hnmod`
- Version: `1.0.1`
- Starts disabled in Options > Mods.

Freecam+ detaches the camera from the player. The camera can move independently with WASD, Space, Shift and Ctrl, and its look direction is controlled separately from the player's rotation. The mouse wheel adjusts flight speed while Freecam+ is active. The settings page controls collision through walls and movement speed.

Android wiring includes the camera-view override, look-turn override, raw keyboard capture, mapped-key input, toggle, tick movement, wheel speed, mod-list registration and settings callbacks.
